Everything in this section is intended to help you troubleshoot issues with lsfg-vk, as well as document common issues and known problems.

If you have a question or an issue not covered here, you can ask for help in the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/5cCP6aACgT) or create a [GitHub discussion](https://github.com/PancakeTAS/lsfg-vk/discussions).
Please keep in mind that support is provided by volunteers in their spare time and I personally cannot possibly look into every post or discussion and provide support to everyone.
